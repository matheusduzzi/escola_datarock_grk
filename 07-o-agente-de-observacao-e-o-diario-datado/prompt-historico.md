# O prompt histórico da sessão

Este é o prompt das sessões de navegação do Mini Assistente, do jeito que o case guarda: em inglês, com os nomes trocados por marcadores. Os treze perfis são marcadores. A lista original não foi reconstituída. O "Chrome do meu computador" descreve o ambiente do case, que era o navegador do box.

```text
Run one natural "warm-up" browsing session on the Instagram test account @conta_teste (already logged in in my computer's Chrome) so Instagram learns its taste. Use the browser through a desktop subagent. The taste is defined by these followed accounts: [@perfil_referencia_1, @perfil_referencia_2, @perfil_referencia_3, @perfil_referencia_4, @perfil_referencia_5, @perfil_referencia_6, @perfil_referencia_7, @perfil_referencia_8, @perfil_referencia_9, @perfil_referencia_10, @perfil_referencia_11, @perfil_referencia_12, @perfil_referencia_13], plus the niche accounts the teammate "Roteirista" follows (listed in /workspace/criadora-roteiros/contas-seguidas.md; read it at the start and include a couple of them). Each session (~10-15 minutes, human pace, varied pauses, vary what you do between sessions): visit 3-5 of those profiles at random, open recent posts/reels and stories; like a few posts (about 6-12 likes total per session, never every post, and make sure each like registered by checking the heart turned red, never clicking comment hearts); scroll the home feed a bit; ALWAYS open Explore and the Reels tab and watch ~10-15 reels, letting good ones play most of their length. Steer the algorithm toward the Creator's content positioning: real-life women's fitness and routine (gym workouts, strengthening for core/lower back/shins, CrossFit, Hyrox, spinning, Pilates, practical everyday meals/snacks, spontaneous daily life and behind-the-scenes, without posing as a pro athlete, personal trainer, or recipe/nutrition specialist). In Explore and Reels, spend more time on and like 2-4 reels of those themes, and skip quickly past running/Strava content and anything off-niche. Using Instagram search for terms like "treino feminino", "hyrox", "crossfit feminino", "pilates", "rotina real treino" also helps. Also watch for creative supplement/gummy unboxing or partnership launch reels. Every session, follow the Creator's day: open her profile, watch stories/new posts (liking 1-2 of her new posts is fine). Append a short dated note to /workspace/criadora-roteiros/dia-a-dia/<YYYY-MM-DD>.md so the Roteirista can use it. Save standout viral reels to referencias-nutricao.md with author and view/like count. Hard rules: NEVER comment; never DM, follow/unfollow, share, post, or change settings. Close popups. On action block, 'Try again later', checkpoint, verification code, or logout, stop immediately. Message the manager only if blocked/logged out/verification; otherwise send nothing.
```

Duas notas do próprio case sobre esse texto:

- "Follow the Creator's day" quer dizer acompanhar o dia da Criadora, não clicar em Seguir.
- "ALWAYS open Explore" não vence a ordem de parar por segurança nem pede insistência sem fim numa tela que não carrega.

Você não vai rodar esse prompt. Ele é o ponto de partida da reescrita.
